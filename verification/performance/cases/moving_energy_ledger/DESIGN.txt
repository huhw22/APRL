Moving-volume energy accounting (2026-10-29)

Scope
-----
Read-only measurements around the existing field advance, inlet assignment,
remap and particle deposition. Cowan, Boris, CPML, reference values, release
events, deposit arguments and update ordering are unchanged. All energies are
boost-frame quantities; this is not a radiation-only or lab-spectrum budget.

Energy functional and signs
---------------------------
U uses the existing cell-centred E/B interpolation and the physical volume
inside transverse CPML. K includes pending and retained particles; removed
particle kinetic energy remains in the accounted K total. Positive W adds
energy; positive Q is outward.

R_raw = delta(K_retained + K_removed + U) + sum(Q_face) - W_prescribed.
R = R_raw - W_ballistic_boundary - deltaU_remap - W_inlet
          - W_frozen_constraint - W_carrier_constraint.
Q_longitudinal = Q_zminus + Q_zplus - deltaU_remap - W_inlet.
deltaU_remap = U_inserted - U_cropped + U_interface_correction.

The inserted/cropped terms are components of remap, not additional terms in R.
The six Q_face values represent propagation only. No duplicate -u*v sweep
flux is added to the explicitly measured remap.

Field sequence
--------------
At beginStep capture all six powers at the current fixed window location.
The boundary callback follows electric/CPML updates but precedes MPI electric
interface completion. It measures the inlet Ex/Ey assignment's change of the
existing cell-centred energy and its local change in boundary power. After
MPI completion, the full end power minus that assignment contribution gives
the pre-assignment propagation endpoint. Trapezoid integration joins endpoints
at one location, not the previous step's pre-remap boundary.

Only cell 0 uses the replaced Ex/Ey node plane. Its energy difference includes
unchanged neighbour nodes and their cross terms. It is not the squared energy
of the replacement field or difference field. Remap retained cells map exactly
to old cells. Each rank measures entering minus leaving cell energy; MPI
interface discrepancies are explicitly retained, not assumed to cancel.
Independent module tests compare these measurements to full-volume sums.

Constrained sources
-------------------
Frozen particles and post-exit current carriers can deposit without changing
their accounted kinetic energy. Actual locally deposited segments are clipped
to the physical volume. Their separate supply-work estimate is
  W = -sum(q_effective * dx dot (E_old + E_new)/2).
Carriers use their actual deposited current weight. Each sample uses the path
midpoint; new E receives the same MPI interpolation halo as old E. All ranks
participate in this diagnostic halo exchange, including empty-sample ranks.
This estimate is independent of the measured residual. It is NOT an exact
adjoint of CIC deposition, and partial-step segments use Maxwell-step endpoint
fields. It must remain labelled approximate, separate from device work.

Limits and cost
---------------
Cell-centred E and staggered B, Cowan spatial filtering, particle/source
approximations and approximate surface integration do not define an exact
algebraic invariant. closure_valid retains its conservative existing tests;
recording missing exchanges does not itself certify physical convergence.
Tests report the residual without choosing a new percentage to call it PASS.

Disabled diagnostics add no measurement scans, sample allocation, timers or
communication. Enabled measurements traverse surfaces/boundary cells and
actual constrained trajectories; no additional full-grid array is allocated.
Two rank-seconds counters report boundary/remap and constrained-source costs.
End-to-end profile-off paired timings, rather than these counters alone, are
the performance evidence. New optional diagnostics are not a reason to alter
the production window, timestep, input, default binary or simulation length.
