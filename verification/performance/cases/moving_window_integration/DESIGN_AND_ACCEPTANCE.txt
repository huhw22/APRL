2026-10-28: integrated moving-window first version

Implementation
- Opt-in build APRL_ENABLE_MOVING_WINDOW (default OFF), opt-in
  mesh.moving_window.enabled (default false). FFTW is needed only by this build.
- Simulation first reconstructs the existing held input. The original mesh is
  the finite reference/source domain. Rank zero generates disk-backed scalar
  blocks with the accepted Cowan reference; other ranks open read-only readers.
  No full-domain E/B donor is allocated. Every field grid and transverse CPML
  history belongs to a fixed-size z slab of the compact active window.
- Motion is one dz toward -z per complete Cowan step (c*dt=dz), using integer
  world offsets. Lorentz frame origins stay fixed. Native E(n), B(n-1/2) and
  transverse CPML histories move together; the inserted history plane is zero.
- Left E/B are supplied by the translated common-beta reference. The rightmost
  field collar is discarded causally; no longitudinal CPML is introduced.
  Fixed-lab detectors recompute their grid position/owner after each shift.
- Physical particles leave at a fixed lab plane beyond all requested elements
  and diagnostics, inside the causal collar. Existing undamped current carriers
  complete deposition until the right crop. No artificial z current taper.
- MPI particle sampling in moving mode exchanges the missing half-z Ez/Bx/By
  neighbours after B and clears the view after all particle pushes/transfers.
  Original trilinear weights, Cowan, Boris and charge deposition are unchanged.
  The legacy fixed-window sampling path retains its previous behaviour.
- Complete source input is retained in this version, as requested. Pending
  upstream held particles are currently owned by the inlet rank. Large-count
  initialization uses 64 MiB packets, avoiding MPI int byte displacement limits.
  This is not a distributed particle-streaming or long-bunch throughput solution.
  The inlet rank's full pending list can dominate a much larger case; the small
  timing figures must not be extrapolated to 41,732,775 particles / 256 ranks.
- Checkpoint output/restart is rejected with moving mode. Legacy checkpoint code
  is retained. Retirement, incident waves, ballistic transverse injection,
  padded source solves, nonzero z CPML and current filtering are rejected here.
  Held longitudinal velocities must be nonnegative; MPI slabs need >=8 cells.
- Existing shutdown watchdog remains. Output directories are now established
  and opened on every client, with bounded ENOENT/ESTALE retry and collective
  failure propagation; a barrier alone did not ensure immediate visibility.

    upper_guard_cells: 128

# output directories, relative to the working directory when not absolute.

Automatic width includes the lab release-to-last-interaction span, 64/128 guards
and two native endpoint cells. No fs-scale quiet tail or safety duration is added.
Actual offset/width/exit and reference beta are logged, with per-rank moving-
volume energy and exit accounting in reference/window-rank-*.csv. The old fixed-
volume ledger residual alone is not moving-volume closure or radiation energy.

Physical and cost limits
Dense single-node physical pair (same node/layout/input): field nz 896 -> 289;
steady RSS 1846.53 -> 742.82 MiB; measured step 0.52905 -> 0.24982 seconds.
This is work reduction through a shorter field domain, not a same-grid kernel
benchmark or a repeated production performance measurement. Startup also changes
from CG to the already tested blocked reference; these gains are not counted
again as an optimization of the Cowan kernel.
Dense exit DeltaK RMS=0.00723 eV; auxiliary self-field width-increment difference
~0.00069%; 91.5 eV bunching amplitude difference <6.2e-12.
The full-time physical-aperture E/B L2 difference is ~1.482%. Weak late fields
retain localized differences up to ~39.3% near the transverse CPML physical
edge, and ~9.67% at the maximum-error core pixel. Relative late error can grow
while its absolute RMS remains approximately level; no instability is inferred.
CIC/reference translation and inserted CPML history are candidates for separate
controlled work, not proven exclusive causes. No CPML or reference-method change
was made to hide these differences. Total E/B is not a radiation-only field.
