Energy diagnostics: two independent frames
==========================================

Keep energy_ledger.enabled: true for the boost moving-volume audit. The v5 file
retains the legacy prescribed_work_J and balance_residual_J for comparison.
Use balance_residual_boris_J for the new work audit, and inspect the entire
record sequence rather than only the final residual.

  build/energy-transport-audit-20261029/energy_ledger_report LEDGER.h5 [INDEX]

The split is:
  particle_work_balance_residual = delta_K_box - W_grid - W_prescribed - dK_magnetic
  field_exchange_residual = delta_U + outward + W_grid - imposed_sources
  balance_residual_boris = particle_work_balance_residual
                         + field_exchange_residual + dK_magnetic
imposed_sources contains ballistic, remap, inlet-overwrite, frozen and carrier
terms. The grid/prescribed electric works come directly from the actual Boris
kicks, independently of these balance residuals. A terminal fraction inside a
Boris substep uses a linear prefix and is counted by partial_work_substeps.
particle_removal_interpolation_delta_J separates the change from the legacy
whole-step terminal interpolation. A previously uncounted cross-rank release
and terminal event in the same step is also included by the new bookkeeping.

The particle lab change is recorded directly, including removed particles,
and checked against Gamma * (delta_K_box + beta * delta_cPz_box). cPz has units
of joules. Particle inventory in this diagnostic lies on a common boost-time
slice; it is not a simultaneous lab-volume inventory. No energy-only boost
factor guess is required. Pending particles use cached block/suffix totals.

For fixed lab transport add independent planes using the input card's usual
length/time units, for example in an SI card:

  detectors:
    directory: /absolute/new/output/detectors
    energy_planes:
      - {name: entry, z: -0.05, rhythm: 1.0e-15, buffer_samples: 64}
      - {name: exit, z: 0.06, rhythm: 1.0e-15, buffer_samples: 64}

This creates entry-energy.h5 and exit-energy.h5. No raw field_planes or
particle_planes are required. Empty/absent energy_planes disables the feature.
Positions must be chosen for the actual simulation; the example belongs only
to the bounded acceptance fixture. Existing raw detector files are unchanged.

Field power is the signed lab integral of (E x B)_z / mu0 over the plane.
It is sampled at every Maxwell step and integrated in time by trapezoids;
rhythm controls only file writing. Full and physical transverse apertures are
separate. The latter excludes transverse CPML cells. E/B retain their original
Yee time staggering; prescribed device fields are not part of self-field flux.
field_energy_per_length_lab_J_per_m means integral of field energy density over
area, in J/m; it is not an enclosed field-energy inventory in J.

Particle crossings use actual Boris substep endpoints, interpolating energy
and longitudinal momentum as separate scalars. This avoids the artificial
energy drop caused by interpolating the momentum vector then taking its norm.
The interpolated diagnostic is not an on-shell particle snapshot. Mass/charge
already include macro weight. Signed and absolute crossing counts and physical
aperture totals are retained. Accumulators expose high/low double parts;
reconstruct in long double before subtracting large entry/exit energies.

A complete file does not imply full field coverage. Check field_available,
field_sample_count, outside_field_window_steps, field_gap_count and first/last
field times. Counts include the initial step-zero sample; gaps count recovery
after unavailable intervals. No integration bridges missing intervals.
Particle totals alone do not prove identical IDs or physical-aperture cohorts.

All field quantities are total self-consistent E/B, not isolated near fields
or radiation. Entry/exit particle-plus-field transport is a useful comparison,
not a closed lab control-volume balance: transverse flux, stored energy and
sources for that same spacetime volume would also be needed. The boost ledger
provides the separate full-volume balance. No diagnostic pass upgrades
closure_valid or establishes the physical origin of a production energy loss.

This version rejects old native checkpoint ABI v1; energy_planes plus any
checkpoint/restart is rejected explicitly. Snapshot work is outside this task.
