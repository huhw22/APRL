Energy-transport acceptance contract

Two separate coordinate/event contracts are mandatory:
1. Boost moving-volume ledger: field inventory, propagation through all six
   faces, remap/overwrite supply, constrained sources and all particle kinetic
   energy including removed particles. New pusher-work and momentum identities
   are independent diagnostics. No residual is assigned to an invented work term.
2. Fixed-lab energy planes: signed +z particle transport and Poynting integral,
   full and physical xy apertures separately. Surface integral of energy density
   has units J/m, not J, and is not added to a particle crossing energy.

Each energy plane writes /energy_plane/records with transactional
committed_records/complete markers. The high/low energy and cpz fields must be
recombined in long double. Particle rest energy and signed charge provide an
independent consistency check of total minus kinetic energy. Lab/boost total E
and cpz for the same events must satisfy the Lorentz transform. Current and
removed particle states must never be confused with a matching crossing cohort.

Incoming/outgoing cumulative K is compared only after particle counts, charge,
direction and event coverage are checked. New energy-plane values are checked
independently; unchanged legacy field/particle/trajectory files are compared
byte-for-byte after source-ID sorting where legacy append ordering requires it.

field_available, field_sample_count, outside_field_window_steps and field_gap_count
describe coverage. Complete=1 only means the writer closed normally. A missing
interval is not implicitly zero field and is not bridged by the reviewer.
Where every consecutive field sample is written, recompute signed trapezoidal
energy from recorded powers and lab times; otherwise mark that specific check
unavailable rather than relabel cadence-limited output as an independent full
flux integration. Particle accounting is independent of field coverage.

For a lab-plane pair, the diagnostic transport difference is
  [K_in + integral(Sz_in)] - [K_out + integral(Sz_out)].
It is not called an energy-conservation residual: lateral flux, simultaneous
interior-field change and source work over the same control volume are not
automatically supplied by two crossing planes. Total field is not pure near
field. The review never mixes this quantity with a boost scalar energy sum.

Software acceptance: all outputs finite and transaction-complete, expected
cohort coverage, exact unchanged legacy states, reconstructable identities,
normal physical termination and MPI finalization. Numerical bounds for
identity reconstruction use original source magnitudes and floating-point
precision; they are not allowed physical error percentages. Zero physical
denominators are reported as null, not silently replaced by total beam energy.

Physics reporting: plot/report the whole time series and the largest imbalance,
its step and denominator, as well as terminal values; separate kernel work
identity, grid exchange, boundary supply and event reconstruction errors. No
closure_valid promotion, no winner-dependent retry and no dropped timing sample.
